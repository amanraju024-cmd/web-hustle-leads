Questions

Good questions we get asked.

Do I need to write anything or know about tech?

No. We ask you a few questions on the phone and write everything for you. You just check it before it goes live.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, what you do, where you work and how people contact you. A logo and some photos help, but they're not required.

I already have a Facebook or Instagram page. Do I still need a website?

Your socials are great for people who already follow you. A website is what shows up when new customers search on Google, and it links back to your socials.

Can you redo my old website?

Yes. If your current website doesn't work well on phones or hasn't been touched in years, we'll rebuild it properly.

What happens if I need changes later?

On a care plan (from $10 a month) we make small changes for you. Without one, just ask and we'll quote you.

Where are you based?
