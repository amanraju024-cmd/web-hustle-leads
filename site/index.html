FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Anything else? Call 0493 523 482.

Do I need to write anything or know about tech?

No. We ask you a few questions and write everything for you. You just check it before it goes live.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, what you do, where you work and how people contact you. A logo and photos help, but they're not required.

I already have Facebook or Instagram. Do I still need a website?

Your socials are great for people who already follow you. A website is what shows up when new customers search on Google, and it links back to your socials.

Can you redo my old website?

Yes. If your current site doesn't work well on phones or hasn't been touched in years, we'll rebuild it properly.

How do the NFC cards work?

Each card or stand has a tiny chip and a QR code linked to your page. Customers tap their phone on it (or scan the code) and your Google review page, Facebook or Instagram opens instantly. Works with iPhone and Android.

What if I need changes later?

On a care plan (from $10 a month) we make small changes for you. Without one, just ask and we'll quote you.
